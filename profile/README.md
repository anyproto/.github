### Trust our code, not our words
At the Any Association, we believe fundamental freedoms can be built into software. You should be able to keep your thoughts private, speak freely, and be recognized as the author of your work. If the Association closed its doors tomorrow, you should still be able to open what you made today.

These beliefs shape how we build our protocols, libraries, and apps. Keeping data on your device lets you work offline. When you share a document, end-to-end encryption lets a server pass it along without being able to read it. A digital signature lets a collaborator verify that an edit was signed with your key. Explore the Any database

We publish the source so you can inspect how these choices work, build the software yourself, and use it on your own terms. You can also run your own sync network or build an application on the same protocols. Explore our repositories · Run your own sync network

We welcome contributions across our projects. You can build an integration, design a theme, share something you’ve made, improve a guide, or bring a project to more people by translating it into your language.
